# Lead Insights Mentorcircle

Build a premium, fully responsive corporate website for **Lead Insights Africa Consulting**, founded and led by **Jane Mburia**, with **MentorCircle Conversations** presented as its leadership, coaching and personal-development pillar.

This is a Nairobi-based consulting firm serving Kenya, East Africa, the wider African region, and international organisations entering or expanding in Africa. The website must feel credible to C-suite leaders, investors, boards, multinational organisations, local corporates, SMEs, and ambitious professionals.

Use the uploaded logos from the supplied presentations exactly as brand assets. Do not redraw, alter, or replace them. Lead Insights Africa Consulting is the parent brand. MentorCircle Conversations is a clearly connected but distinct sub-brand.

## Brand direction

Create a refined, editorial, global-consulting aesthetic inspired by the clarity and confidence of leading firms such as McKinsey, BCG, Bain, and Accenture, while remaining distinctly African and human.

- Tone: credible, composed, insightful, senior, warm, and practical.
- Avoid generic startup design, loud gradients, stock-office clichés, excessive animations, glassmorphism, cluttered cards, or fake statistics.
- Use the Lead Insights purple logo palette as the core visual anchor: deep aubergine, confident purple, warm off-white, charcoal, and restrained metallic-gold accents where appropriate.
- Use MentorCircle’s pink accent only within MentorCircle pages and calls to action.
- Typography: elegant modern serif for major headlines and a clean professional sans-serif for body copy. Use generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, and large editorial photography.
- Photography should show authentic executive leadership, meaningful conversations, African business environments, city detail, teams, and mentorship. Avoid safari imagery, flags, maps as decoration, and stereotypical “Africa” visuals.
- Use subtle line patterns, abstract network motifs, or architectural details to suggest connection, strategy, and momentum.
- The website must look exceptional on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Technical requirements

Build with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and accessible semantic HTML. Create a polished production-ready frontend with reusable components, responsive navigation, fast loading, image optimisation, smooth but restrained transitions, and WCAG-conscious contrast and keyboard navigation.

Create these working features:

- A responsive header with dropdown navigation and a mobile menu.
- Contact and enquiry forms with validation, clear error states, loading states, and confirmation messages.
- Forms should submit through a secure `/api/contact` endpoint. Configure email delivery to `jmburia@leadinsightsafrica.com` through environment variables, such as Resend or Nodemailer. Never expose keys in the browser.
- A “Request a conversation” modal available throughout the site.
- Service enquiry fields: name, organisation, email, phone, country, area of interest, message, and consent checkbox.
- MentorCircle programme-interest form with individual/corporate options.
- CMS-ready data files for services, insights, programmes, and team content. Do not invent case studies, client logos, testimonials, awards, or performance results.
- SEO metadata, Open Graph images, sitemap, robots file, structured organisation schema, and page-specific titles/descriptions.
- Use accessible alt text for all images.
- Integrate LinkedIn for Jane Mburia using a visible external link. Do not invent any other social links.

## Content and editorial rules

- Write in British English.
- Be clear, assured and specific.
- Do not use phrases such as “world-class,” “game-changing,” “unlock potential,” “future-proof,” or “transformative solutions” unless supported by evidence.
- Do not invent projects, clients, partners, testimonials, logos, certifications, awards, office locations, media features or statistics.
- Make Lead Insights feel established, senior and approachable.
- Keep MentorCircle warm, thoughtful and aspirational without becoming informal or overly feminine.
- Ensure every page has a clear next action.
